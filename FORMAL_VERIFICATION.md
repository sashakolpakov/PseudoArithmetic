# Formal verification boundary

Release candidate: `1.0.0-rc1`

The project in [`formal/`](formal/README.md) is pinned to Lean and mathlib
`v4.32.0`. It formalizes the manuscript's internal implication graph while
leaving cited geometry and classification results as explicit local
hypotheses. It constructs the displayed matrices and their cyclic-product
number fields, proves non-total reality for all three, and checks the
subsequent conditional implications.
The geometric predicates are abstract: no Coxeter polytope, reflection
group, or hyperbolic manifold is constructed in Lean.

## Unconditional manuscript results

[`Manuscript.lean`](formal/PseudoArithmetic/Manuscript.lean) proves:

1. `recover_s_from_twoCycle`: from
   $c_{12}=(3+s)/2$, recover $s=2c_{12}-3$;
2. `recover_u_from_threeCycle`: from $c_{035}=-2u$, recover
   $u=-c_{035}/2$;
3. `cycle_relation_of_radical_identities`: the final ring-theoretic
   elimination in the nested-radical calculation;
4. `explicit_radical_cycle_relation`: for the actual positive radicals

   $$
   s=\sqrt5,\quad
   a=\frac12\sqrt{7+s+2\sqrt{2+2s}},\quad
   u=\sqrt2a,
   $$

   prove

   $$
   (s-1)u^2+2(s-3)u+s-7=0;
   $$

5. `TwoCycleCertificate.of_real_cycles`: given a number field $K$, a real
   embedding $\iota$, and elements $c_{12},c_{035}\in K$ with
   $\iota(c_{12})=(3+\sqrt5)/2$ and
   $\iota(c_{035})=-2\sqrt2a$, recover $s,u\in K$ and prove both the
   minimal-polynomial statement and the radical relation;
6. `not_totallyReal_of_real_cycles`: conclude that $K$ is not totally real
   from those two cycle values alone.

The certificate records four elements and four equations. Its constructor
connects the real radical calculation to the abstract number field by the
injectivity of $\iota$. The source-data scripts do not supply Lean proofs.

[`NonTotalReality.lean`](formal/PseudoArithmetic/NonTotalReality.lean) proves
the field-theoretic core:

- `minpoly_eq_of_square_five`: $s^2=5$ implies
  $\mathrm{minpoly}_{\mathbf Q}(s)=X^2-5$ in a characteristic-zero field;
- the conjugate quadratic has no real zero when $s^2=5$ and $s<0$;
- a complex embedding carrying $s$ to $-\sqrt5$ cannot be real; and
- for a number field $K$ and $s,u\in K$,

  $$
  \mathrm{minpoly}_{\mathbf Q}(s)=X^2-5,
  \qquad
  (s-1)u^2+2(s-3)u+s-7=0
  $$

  imply that $K$ is not totally real.

The last theorem uses mathlib's number-field embedding theorem to obtain an
embedding with $s\mapsto-\sqrt5$ from the minimal polynomial. The bad
embedding is a conclusion, not an input.
The variant `not_totallyReal_of_cycle_square` needs only $s^2=5$ and the
quadratic relation, since irreducibility is now proved internally.

## Worked semantic audit

[`SemanticAudit.lean`](formal/PseudoArithmetic/SemanticAudit.lean) constructs
the number field generated by the actual radicals and applies both the
algebraic theorem and the concrete cycle constructor to it. It proves
counterexamples to deleting either equation, allowing the root to live
outside the field, and confusing non-total reality with total complexity.
It also proves the exact real-root boundary and infinite families of roots
and non-roots for the quadratic.

The [worked audit](notes/two_cycle_semantic_audit.md) maps the English
clauses to the Lean signature and explains each negative control. A separate
exact-rational script checks 28,310 cases, including perturbed roots and
boundary cases. The release validator reruns it and compares its report.
The [manuscript-wide register](notes/manuscript_semantic_audit.md) now covers
135 statement entries across all 145 source blocks, with a positive example
and a negative control for every entry. Translation assessment is separate
from proof provenance. External inputs are accepted when their specialization,
hypotheses, and use match the English statements; library reuse or a different
checked proof is also acceptable. Written geometric examples are labeled
separately from executed checks. The
[external translation review](notes/external_translation_audit.md) explains
the seven contracts and the original 21 partial entries: 13 are now explicit
checked statements, and 8 retain imported geometric or
adjoint interpretations.

The [subdiagram and face audit](notes/subdiagram_face_semantic_audit.md)
completes the two previously separate local computations in Lean. Exact
matrix congruences and negative vectors classify all 127 nonempty principal
subsets of P₁₁,₈ and prove the complete maximal-vertex list. The face modules
reconstruct closed incidences, prove stabilization of chain dimension, and
count all five dimensions to obtain (10,21,18,7,1). Geometric transfer assumes
only a complete, dimension-preserving face correspondence, with no supplied
counts. Controls cover 70 incidence flips, ten vertex deletions, nonsimple
closure, and infinitely many wrong vectors satisfying Euler's formula.

[`GramAudit.lean`](formal/PseudoArithmetic/GramAudit.lean) defines all three
matrices, all closed products, and the fields they generate inside ℝ. It
proves algebraicity and number-field instances, the two cycle values and
recovered generators, non-total reality of all three fields, and equality
of the third cycle field with both its entry field and the literal four-generator
adjoin. A strict cycle/entry-field counterexample is also proved. It checks rational normalization,
closed-cycle reversal, and reflection-coefficient rescaling.

The other audit modules prove the field preliminaries, the arbitrary complex
solution version of the obstruction, the total-reality/total-positivity
equivalence, the five leading-minor identities, roots-of-unity identities,
Lorentz reflection identities, trace calculations, orientation
indices, finite-index finite generation, and noncompactness under continuous
surjection. Explicit Boolean models test the conditional interfaces without
claiming to be geometric examples.

The independent exact matrix audit checks all three ranks and inertias,
127 principal subdiagrams, and 69 targeted mutations: 24 cycle perturbations,
32 power-basis coordinate perturbations, 10 vertex deletions, and 3 rank
perturbations. PARI controls verify the field assertions and demonstrate that
degree, signature, and discriminant alone do not determine a number field.

## Manuscript-facing external boundary

[`ExternalContracts.lean`](formal/PseudoArithmetic/ExternalContracts.lean)
specializes the imported statements to hyperbolic dimension four.
`CensusInputs` relates each group to its actual `gram j`, requires finite
volume and noncompactness separately, and supplies Vinberg's equality with
the generated cyclic field. Its pseudo-arithmetic implication requires the
lattice hypothesis and concerns the same group's field.
`census_main_theorem` combines these contracts with the actual matrix-field
proofs. No caller-selected field or certificate is required.

`CoverInputs` supplies one subgroup/quotient pair satisfying finite index,
torsion-freeness, orientation, and quotient correspondence simultaneously.
Finite-index invariance is equality of the two field values. The geometry
contract supplies the cover's lattice property as well as the manifold
geometry, so the pseudo-arithmetic implication applies with all hypotheses
intact. `census_manifold_corollary` proves the required existence statement
and identifies the cover's field with the same concrete `cycleField j`.

Positive models use the actual matrix fields. Negative controls reject a
wrong case/matrix pair, an unrelated totally real field, reversed implication
or relation direction, and incompatible witnesses. The vocabulary still
has an external geometric interpretation; these logical models do not
construct hyperbolic groups or manifolds. The earlier `CoxeterExternalInputs`
and `ManifoldExternalInputs` remain reusable abstract interfaces.

These inputs are structure fields supplied to individual theorems. They are
not global Lean axioms.

## External statements not newly verified

The project does not formalize from first principles:

- the Ma--Zheng census or its finite-volume classification;
- the source-file transcription of the three published rows (the equality
  of the decoded Lean rows with the actual Lean matrices is proved);
- Vinberg's identification of the cyclic-product field with the adjoint
  trace field;
- Emery--Mila's theorem that a pseudo-arithmetic trace field is totally real;
- Selberg's lemma;
- the hyperbolic orbifold/manifold quotient construction; or
- finite-index invariance of the adjoint trace field.

The exact arithmetic scripts verify the concrete transcription and radical
relations independently, but script execution is not conflated with kernel
verification of the cited theorems.
Their imported proof status is intentional and is not a translation defect.

## Conditional supplementary development

[`ConditionalDescent.lean`](formal/PseudoArithmetic/ConditionalDescent.lean)
checks the repository's separate normalized-Clifford and split-support proof
graph. The following classical inputs are ordinary hypotheses:

- normalization of odd-rank similarity descent;
- Clifford-class necessity;
- local Brauer restriction;
- ABHN/Chebotarev sufficiency;
- quadratic-form realization and Hasse--Minkowski; and
- the pseudo-arithmetic candidate characterization.

From those inputs Lean proves the complete conditional criterion, its
necessary lightweight direction, and the incompatibility of a nonsplit or
incomplete ramified orbit with similarity descent. The candidate type is
abstract; its finiteness and enumeration are not formalized. This module is
not used by the manuscript's two-cycle proof.

## Build and source audit

Run:

```sh
cd formal
lake build
```

The release validator also checks every project `.lean` file for forbidden
declarations or proof escapes, excluding dependencies under `.lake/`.
The project contains no declaration of `axiom` or `opaque` and no use of
`sorry`, `admit`, or `native_decide` in its proofs. The validator also runs
`#print axioms` on every declaration named in the statement register, as well
as the principal algebraic and conditional declarations, and
rejects dependencies beyond `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`.

As usual, the trusted base consists of the Lean kernel, the pinned Lean
compiler and mathlib declarations, and Lean's standard logical foundations.
