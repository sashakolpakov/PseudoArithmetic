# Three non-pseudo-arithmetic Coxeter lattices in dimension four

Status: incorporated into the `1.0.0-rc1` manuscript on 24 September 2026.
This note is retained as the concise discovery record. The algebraic
certificate below is exact and hand-checkable. Ma--Zheng's published census
supplies the finite-volume polytopes; a separate local computation
independently checks the Gram rank, inertia, and vertex links for $P_{11,8}$.

## Theorem

Let $P_{11,j}$, for $j=6,7,8$, be the finite-volume hyperbolic Coxeter
$4$-polytopes in the Ma--Zheng seven-facet census, and let
$\Gamma_{11,j}<\mathrm{PO}(4,1)$ be their reflection groups. Then the
adjoint trace field of every $\Gamma_{11,j}$ is not totally real. In
particular, none of the three lattices is pseudo-arithmetic.

Consequently there are finite-volume, noncompact, orientable hyperbolic
$4$-manifolds that are not pseudo-arithmetic. Thus Questions 1.8 and 1.10 of
Emery--Mila both have negative answers.

## Common Coxeter data

The three rows of the census are:

| polytope | Coxeter vector | cusps | $\mathrm{vol}(P)/\pi^2$ |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| $P_{11,6}$ | `2232ab 52322 2220 442 22 2` | 1 | $13/864$ |
| $P_{11,7}$ | `2232ab 52323 2220 442 22 2` | 1 | $1/54$ |
| $P_{11,8}$ | `2232ab 52324 2220 442 22 2` | 2 | $19/864$ |

They share the first dotted Gram entry

$$
a=\frac12\sqrt{7+\sqrt5+2\sqrt{2+2\sqrt5}}.
$$

Write $s=\sqrt5$ and $r=\sqrt2$. Let $G$ be the normalized Gram matrix,
with $g_{ii}=1$, and put $A=2G$. The adjoint trace field of a finite-volume
Coxeter reflection group is its Vinberg field

$$
k(P)=\mathbf Q\bigl(\text{cyclic products of }A\bigr).
$$

This distinction matters: the field generated by the individual entries of
$G$ need not equal the adjoint trace field.

## Two-cycle proof

Use zero-based facet indices. The common labeled subgraph has

$$
A_{12}=-\frac{1+s}{2},\qquad
A_{03}=-1,\qquad A_{35}=-r,\qquad A_{50}=-2a.
$$

Therefore two cyclic products in each Vinberg field $K_j=k(P_{11,j})$ are

$$
c_{12}=A_{12}A_{21}=\frac{3+s}{2},
\qquad
c_{035}=A_{03}A_{35}A_{50}=-2ra.
$$

It follows immediately that

$$
s=2c_{12}-3\in K_j,
\qquad
u=ra=-\frac12c_{035}\in K_j.
$$

The displayed value of $a$—equivalently, the isolated rank-five Gram
equation—gives

$$
(s-1)u^2+2(s-3)u+s-7=0. \tag{1}
$$

The discriminant of (1) over $\mathbf Q(s)$ is

$$
\Delta=8(1+s).
$$

Under the conjugate real embedding $s\mapsto-s$, it becomes
$8(1-s)<0$. Hence that embedding of $\mathbf Q(s)$ extends to non-real
embeddings of $\mathbf Q(s,u)$. (In particular, $u\notin\mathbf Q(s)$,
since otherwise its conjugate would be a real root of the conjugate
polynomial.) Thus

$$
\mathbf Q(s,u)\subset K_j
$$

has a complex place. Every embedding of this subfield into $\mathbf C$
extends to $K_j$, so $K_j$ is not totally real.

Emery--Mila observe directly from their definition that the adjoint trace
field of a pseudo-arithmetic lattice in dimension $>3$ is a real
multiquadratic extension of a totally real field, hence is itself totally
real. This contradicts the preceding paragraph and proves the theorem.

Finally, adjoint trace fields and ambient groups are commensurability
invariants. Passing first to the orientation-preserving subgroup and then,
by Selberg's lemma, to a torsion-free finite-index subgroup preserves the
obstruction. The resulting quotient of $\mathbb H^4$ is an orientable
finite-volume manifold rather than only a Coxeter orbifold.

## Exact geometric and field checks

For all three rows, the bounded exact check verifies:

- the source vector and source SHA-256;
- the stated dotted lengths and the exact inequalities $a,b>1$;
- all seven principal $6\times6$ minors equal zero;
- Gram rank $5$;
- a common nonsingular principal block on facets $0,1,2,3,4$, whose leading
  principal minors have signs $+,+,+,+,-$;
- consequently, by Sylvester inertia and the rank equality, exact full Gram
  inertia $(4,1,2)$;
- the two cyclic products above and equation (1).

For $P_{11,8}$ there is an additional whole-field check. Four cycles recover
$s,r,a,b$, so

$$
k(P_{11,8})=\mathbf Q(s,r,a,b).
$$

The primitive element $w=2(r+a+b)$ has irreducible polynomial

$$
\begin{aligned}
p_w(x)={}&x^8-8x^7-252x^6-1392x^5-3524x^4\\
         &-5280x^3-5712x^2-3968x-1136.
\end{aligned}
$$

PARI/GP gives field signature $(r_1,r_2)=(4,2)$ and discriminant
$40960000=2^{16}5^4$. A second primitive-element route gives a different
degree-eight polynomial with the same signature and field discriminant.

The independent $P_{11,8}$ vertex check uses the ten facet brackets of the
$P_{11}$ combinatorial type. It finds eight elliptic rank-four vertex
submatrices and exactly two parabolic rank-three vertices: the simple affine
$\widetilde C_3$ link and the nonsimple
$\widetilde A_1\sqcup\widetilde C_2$ prism link. This matches the two cusps
in the published table.

## Reproducibility

- `scripts/p11_6_7_non_total_trace.py` and
  `results/p11_6_7_non_total_trace_certificate.json` certify $P_{11,6}$ and
  $P_{11,7}$ in fresh workers.
- `scripts/p11_8_non_total_trace.py` and
  `results/p11_8_non_total_trace_certificate.json` give the independent
  full certificate for $P_{11,8}$.
- `scripts/p118_trace_field.py` and
  `results/p118_trace_field_certificate.json` give the second
  primitive-element/ambient-form route.
- `scripts/p118_field_independent.gp` is the direct PARI irreducibility,
  Sturm, signature, and discriminant regression.
- `scripts/p11_8_finite_volume.py` and
  `results/p11_8_finite_volume_validation.json` give the independent exact
  vertex-link and edge-completion check; see
  `notes/p11_8_finite_volume.md`.
- `results/p11_8_finite_volume.coxiter`,
  `results/p11_8_coxiter_validation.output`, and
  `results/p11_8_coxiter_provenance.json` record the independent pinned
  CoxIter run.

The current source pin is Ma--Zheng's `hcp47.tex`, SHA-256
`0017931637f017641d4935450cc2a07d070866d9c749443d9e3ede9f3a39f003`;
the arXiv source archive has SHA-256
`13f2d88ad740669f35b4d1859e68da4d489a1223b5ff5a99e128eaa71838234f`.
The case workers peak below $100$ MB and are never run concurrently.

One sentence in Ma--Zheng's arithmeticity discussion says that a noncompact
finite-volume Coxeter polytope has $k(P)=\mathbf Q$. Read literally, this is
false without the additional hypothesis of arithmeticity: already the
$5$-edge in the three rows above has the two-cycle
$(3+\sqrt5)/2$. The theorem here uses their exact Gram data and
finite-volume classification, not that sentence.

A targeted search through 4 August 2026 found no previous computation of
these Vinberg fields and no previous non-pseudo-arithmetic lattice in
$\mathrm{PO}(n,1)$ for $n>3$. As recently as 2026,
Belolipetsky--Bogachev--Kolpakov--Slavich still stated that all known
examples were pseudo-arithmetic. This is strong evidence of novelty, not a
claim that a bounded literature search proves absolute priority. The safe
wording is: **we know of no previous example**.
