# External-input translation audit

The acceptance criterion is faithful translation and correct use of the
imported mathematics. Reproving the imported geometric theorems in Lean is
outside that criterion. An external input is therefore not an audit failure.
Reusing a checked mathlib theorem, or proving the same statement by a
different argument, is also not a translation defect.

The earlier count of “21 partial formalizations” mixed these distinctions.
The [statement register](manuscript_semantic_audit.md) now records translation
assessment separately from proof provenance. This note explains the review
of the external inputs and the disposition of those 21 entries.

After the follow-up computation audit, **13 of those 21 entries have explicit checked Lean
statements**, and **8 retain
an imported geometric or adjoint-representation interpretation**. The 19
external statements remain acceptable external mathematics. Neither count
is a count of failed translations.

## Contracts used by the main argument

[`ExternalContracts.lean`](../formal/PseudoArithmetic/ExternalContracts.lean)
specializes the external statements to the three seven-facet cases in
hyperbolic dimension four. It keeps the geometric vocabulary abstract while
making the algebraic identifications literal equalities of fields inside ℝ.

Write Γⱼ for the group selected by case j, Gⱼ for its displayed Lean matrix,
and k(Γ) for the field returned by the interpreted adjoint-trace-field
function. The contracts have the following meaning.

| Input | English statement actually used | Lean specialization and checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Each selected row gives a reflection group with the stated unit-normal matrix, finite volume, and a noncompact quotient. | `census_realization j` relates Γⱼ to the actual `gram j`; `finite_volume j` and `noncompact j` are separate premises. All three constructors are quantified over. A changed case/matrix pair is rejected by `wrong_case_matrix_rejected`. The census's total count 331 is background, not an additional theorem needed by the checked implication. |
| E2 | The adjoint trace field of the finite-volume reflection group equals the field generated by closed products in 2G. | `vinberg Γ A` requires both `reflectionGroupOf Γ A` and `finiteVolumeLattice Γ`, and concludes equality with `generatedCycleField (2*A)`. `traceField_eq_cycleField` specializes this to the same j as E1. It is not a freely interpreted predicate about an independently selected field. `unrelated_field_rejected` rules out substituting the totally real field ℚ(√5). |
| E3 | A pseudo-arithmetic hyperbolic lattice in dimension n>3 has totally real adjoint trace field. | `pseudoArithmetic_totallyReal Γ` requires finite covolume and pseudo-arithmeticity and concludes total reality of that Γ's field. Dimension is fixed to 4 throughout the vocabulary, which satisfies n>3. The implication direction is tested: reversing it admits a countermodel. |
| E4 | Embeddings of number fields extend. | `FieldAudit.embedding_extension` is already proved with mathlib, rather than supplied as an external premise. Further declarations check the minimal-polynomial characterization, separability/primitive-element step, existence of complex roots, and evaluation through the polynomial quotient. |
| E5 | Orientation, finite generation, and Selberg yield a torsion-free orientation-preserving finite-index subgroup. | `cover_exists j` is the needed specialized existence statement, bundled with quotient existence from E7. It does not claim to state the general Selberg theorem. One existential pair Λ,M must simultaneously satisfy finite index, torsion/orientation, and quotient correspondence. Separate witnesses for the individual properties do not suffice. |
| E6 | A finite-index subgroup of the base lattice has the same adjoint trace field. | `traceField_invariant Λ Γ` requires `finiteVolumeLattice Γ` and `finiteIndex Λ Γ`, in that argument order, and concludes `k(Λ)=k(Γ)`. The cover field is therefore the same concrete matrix cycle field used for the base obstruction. |
| E7 | The quotient of this subgroup is an orientable, noncompact, finite-volume hyperbolic 4-manifold; quotient and group have matching pseudo-arithmeticity. | `quotient_geometry` requires base finite volume, base noncompactness, the same finite-index relation, torsion-freeness/orientation, and the same quotient pair. It also returns that Λ is a finite-volume lattice, so E3 applies to Λ. `quotient_pseudoArithmetic` transfers the obstruction to M. |

`census_main_theorem` and `census_manifold_corollary` prove the conclusions
from these contracts and the matrix-derived field theorems. The caller
does not supply a separate field or two-cycle certificate. The earlier
general-purpose interfaces remain available, but these specialized contracts
make the application to the manuscript explicit.

The positive test model uses the actual three cycle fields. Negative controls
check a wrong field, a wrong case/matrix pair, a reversed implication,
separate existential witnesses, and reversed arguments in a directed
finite-index relation. These are tests of the translation's structure. They
do not turn the test predicates into geometric groups or manifolds.

The source review checks E3 against Emery–Mila's dimension restriction and
total-reality consequence in [§1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1810.12837).
The manuscript now consistently says **totally real multiquadratic**:
one real embedding does not suffice. For E2/E6, Dotti's
[Proposition 3.6 and Corollary 3.7](https://homeweb.unifr.ch/kellerha/pub/Dotti-article-2023.pdf)
state the cyclic/adjoint-field identification and commensurability
invariance. The contract specializes these to dimension four and keeps the
unit-normal and doubled-Gram conventions consistent.

## Disposition of the original 21 partial entries

| --- | --- | --- |
| G07, G13, G20, G21, G22 | Labels and scalar formulas were formal, while angles, geodesic hyperplanes, and distances remained geometric. | Expected interpretation boundary. The actual matrices and their cyclic fields are now tied to the external realization by E1/E2; the source's geometric interpretation remains the imported input. |
| G18 | The outward half-space inequality was not a named set in Lean. | Added `outwardHalfSpace`, its membership equivalence, and a normal-sign counterexample. Its algebraic definition is explicit; the outward geometric interpretation is the stated convention. |
| G25 | Only the scalar volume check was formal. | E7 supplies the cover/volume theorem. The main argument needs preservation of finite volume, not a formal measure-theoretic proof of the stronger exact degree-times-volume formula. |
| F05 | The minimal-polynomial definition used mathlib without a dedicated wrapper. | Added `minpoly_characterization`, including monicity, positive degree, annihilation, and least degree. Library reuse is fully acceptable. |
| F11, F16, F17 | The embedding theorem was proved, but the written primitive-element/quotient route was not exposed. | Added the primitive-element and separability statement, the complex-root existence statement, and actual evaluation/embedding maps out of `AdjoinRoot`. A different checked proof of the extension theorem was already sufficient. |
| T01, T03 | General adjoint objects were not constructed. | Expected interpretation boundary for the adjoint representation and field. The contract now uses a field-valued function and literal Vinberg/finite-index equalities. It never substitutes defining-representation traces. The existing sl₂ calculation distinguishes those traces. |
| T10 | Only total positivity ⇒ total reality was explicit. | Added the converse and an equivalence for a field generated by square roots of nonzero radicands. The nonzero premise reflects dᵢ∈k×. The link to pseudo-admissible algebraic groups remains E3. |
| C03 | The row encoding and matrix were separate definitions. | Added `decodeGramEntry` and proved that all 21 decoded entries equal the corresponding matrix entries in every case. Reading the published rows themselves remains external source data. |
| C16 | The special-angle result used mathlib instead of reproducing its roots-of-unity derivation. | The actual z=exp(iπ/5), its fifth power, z≠-1, the quotient polynomial, golden-ratio equation, positive root, and excluded z=-1 boundary are now checked. Reusing mathlib for the cosine value remains acceptable. |
| R05 | The strict-inclusion counterexample had only a written parity argument. | Added a symmetric unit-diagonal matrix with off-diagonal -√5 whose entire cycle field is ℚ; its off-diagonal entry is proved absent from that field. This supplies the claimed negative example in Lean. |
| V03 | The five displayed determinant identities were checked by exact computation while their signs were proved in Lean. | Added `leadingGram` and `leading_minor_values`: all five literal determinants of the actual matrices are now proved in Lean. Full rank and inertia retain their independent computational evidence. |
| V06 | The count 127 was proved, while the actual matrices were classified by an external computation. | Lean now proves the exhaustive classification of all 127 actual principal matrices: 54 positive definite, 12 singular positive semidefinite, and 61 with negative directions. The ten maximal nonnegative sets and the ordinary/ideal vertex ranks are proved internally. |
| V12 | Face counts came from the pinned CoxIter run. | Lean now reconstructs the closed incidence poset from the complete Gram-derived vertex list and proves all five counts (10,21,18,7,1). A dimension-preserving geometric face correspondence is the explicit interpretation input. Euler consistency is tested as an insufficient substitute by an infinite family of wrong vectors. |
| V16 | Both inclusions were available, but no theorem named the four-generator adjoin. | Added `third_cycleField_eq_fourGeneratorField`, stating the displayed equality literally. |

Thus the original 21 were not solely consequences of external theorems.
Several were bookkeeping or proof-presentation distinctions, and several
were local statements that have now been made explicit. The remaining
geometric boundaries are intentional external inputs. The two local
computations singled out in that list now have Lean proofs and a
[worked semantic audit](subdiagram_face_semantic_audit.md). Other supplementary
computational assertions retain separate evidence. Neither category should
be reported as a failure merely because its proof is not implemented in
the project.

## What the translation audit certifies

The review checks the objects, domain restrictions, quantifier order,
implication direction, shared witnesses, and the identities used downstream.
The kernel checks the resulting Lean implications and all named mathematical
lemmas. The correspondence between the interpreted geometric vocabulary and
the cited English theorems remains a source review, which examples and
mutation controls support but cannot mechanize by themselves.

Background statements not used as separate premises—such as the full census
classification or the construction of the ambient algebraic group—are marked
as background in the register. Their full generality is not silently claimed
to have a Lean translation merely because a specialized consequence suffices
for the proof.
