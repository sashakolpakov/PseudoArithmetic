# Novelty audit

Audit date: 2026-08-04.

## Result being audited

The manuscript proves that the reflection lattices of the Ma–Zheng
finite-volume hyperbolic Coxeter polytopes

$$
P_{11,6},\qquad P_{11,7},\qquad P_{11,8}
$$

have non-totally-real adjoint trace fields.  Consequently they are not
pseudo-arithmetic.  Passing to finite-index subgroups gives orientable,
noncompact, finite-volume hyperbolic \(4\)-manifolds with the same
obstruction.

The decisive certificate uses only two cyclic products of \(A=2G\).  They
recover \(s=\sqrt5\) and \(u\) with

$$
(s-1)u^2+2(s-3)u+s-7=0.
$$

At \(s\mapsto-\sqrt5\), the discriminant is
\(8(1-\sqrt5)<0\).

## Defensible priority statement

A targeted search through 2026-08-04 found no previously recorded
non-pseudo-arithmetic lattice in \(\mathrm{PO}(n,1)\) for \(n>3\).
The manuscript therefore says:

> We know of no previously recorded non-pseudo-arithmetic lattice in these
> dimensions.

This is a report of the literature search, not a claim that a bounded search
proves absolute priority.

## Separate pseudo-arithmeticity criterion

The accompanying theoretical note gives an all-candidate finite obstruction
to pseudo-arithmeticity in even dimension.  For every eligible elementary
`2`-subgroup of the automorphism group of the adjoint trace field, it combines
the projective real-signature screen with the split support of the type-`B`
Clifford/Tits class.  The defensible novelty statement is:

> Although each algebraic ingredient is classical, this all-candidate
> Clifford-support obstruction to pseudo-arithmeticity does not appear to
> have been recorded previously.

This does **not** claim novelty for the local Brauer calculation or for
quadratic-form descent.  Emery--Mila give a Witt-transfer isometry-descent
criterion; [Voight](https://jvoight.github.io/quat-book.pdf), Chapter 14,
Exercise 19, records the odd-local-degree ramification rule for quaternion
scalar extension; and Proposition 3.15 of
Belolipetsky--Bogachev--Kolpakov--Slavich gives the quadratic split-pair
precursor in hyperbolic dimension three.  The new packaging is the
similarity-invariant, finite, all-descent-fields test for
pseudo-arithmeticity.

## Closest sources checked

| Source | Relevance |
|---|---|
| Emery–Mila, *Hyperbolic manifolds and pseudo-arithmeticity* ([arXiv:1810.12837](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12837)) | Introduces pseudo-arithmeticity; asks whether every lattice in dimension \(>3\) is pseudo-arithmetic and whether every adjoint trace field is totally real.  It also states that pseudo-arithmetic trace fields are totally real and checks the higher-dimensional Coxeter examples then available. |
| Ma–Zheng, *Finite-volume hyperbolic Coxeter 4-dimensional polytopes with 7 facets* ([arXiv:2401.13698](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13698)) | Classifies the 331 finite-volume seven-facet Coxeter \(4\)-polytopes and supplies the exact three rows used here. |
| Belolipetsky–Bogachev–Kolpakov–Slavich, *Subspace stabilisers in hyperbolic lattices* ([arXiv:2105.06897v11](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06897v11)) | Its July 2026 final version still says that all currently known examples in \(\mathrm{PO}(n,1)\), \(n>3\), are pseudo-arithmetic.  This is the most recent explicit baseline found. |
| Guo–Ma–Zang–Zheng, *Several families of incommensurable noncompact hyperbolic Coxeter polytopes* ([arXiv:2607.14715](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14715)) | A July 2026 source using the same modern cyclic-product description of the Vinberg field for finite-volume Coxeter groups. |

Vinberg's original field-of-definition theorem and Emery–Mila's Section 4.1
were used as the primary mathematical sources for identifying the
cyclic-product field with the adjoint trace field.  The newer preprint is
corroborating terminology, not the logical basis of the proof.

## Claims deliberately not made

- The manuscript does not claim an exhaustive computer search of every
  hyperbolic lattice.
- It does not identify the field generated by individual Gram entries with
  the generally smaller cyclic-product field.
- It does not use a numerical root plot as the non-total-reality proof.
- It does not claim that the normalized Clifford/Brauer descent theorem has
  been fully developed from first principles in mathlib.
- It does not claim that matching degree, signature, and discriminant alone
  prove two number fields isomorphic; the second polynomial is linked to the
  same field by its independent Vinberg-form construction.
