# Non-Pseudo-Arithmetic Hyperbolic 4-Manifolds

Release candidate: `1.0.0-rc1` (24 September 2026)

This repository accompanies Alexander Kolpakov's manuscript
**“Non-Pseudo-Arithmetic Hyperbolic 4-Manifolds.”** It gives three explicit
finite-volume lattices in $\mathrm{PO}(4,1)$ that are not
pseudo-arithmetic:

$$
P_{11,6},\qquad P_{11,7},\qquad P_{11,8}.
$$

They are reflection groups of seven-facet Coxeter polytopes in the Ma--Zheng
census. Their adjoint trace fields contain $s=\sqrt5$ and an element $u$
satisfying

$$
(s-1)u^2+2(s-3)u+s-7=0.
$$

At the conjugate place $s\mapsto-\sqrt5$, the discriminant is
$8(1-\sqrt5)<0$. The adjoint trace fields therefore have complex places.
Since a pseudo-arithmetic lattice in dimension greater than three has totally
real adjoint trace field, the three lattices are not pseudo-arithmetic.
Finite-index torsion-free orientation-preserving subgroups give orientable,
noncompact, finite-volume hyperbolic $4$-manifolds with the same obstruction.

## The two-cycle certificate

Let $G$ be the unit-normal Gram matrix and $A=2G$. Two cyclic products common
to all three cases are

$$
c_{12}=\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2},
\qquad
c_{035}=-2\sqrt2\,a,
$$

where

$$
a=\frac12\sqrt{7+\sqrt5+2\sqrt{2+2\sqrt5}}.
$$

Thus $s=2c_{12}-3$ and $u=-c_{035}/2$ belong to the Vinberg cyclic-product
field. The paper derives the displayed quadratic relation directly from the
positive radicals. Vinberg's theorem identifies the cyclic-product field with
the adjoint trace field of the finite-volume reflection lattice.

This distinction is essential: the field generated by individual Gram entries
need not equal the smaller cyclic-product field.

## Lean formalization

The pinned Lean 4/mathlib project under [`formal/`](formal/README.md)
kernel-checks the manuscript's internal logic.

Unconditionally, it proves:

- the exact nested-radical identity;
- recovery of $s$ and $u$ from the two cyclic products;
- impossibility of a real root at the conjugate value of $s$;
- existence of the bad embedding from
  $\mathrm{minpoly}_{\mathbf Q}(s)=X^2-5$ using mathlib's number-field
  embedding theorem; and
- non-total reality of any number field containing the certificate.

The main lattice theorem and the finite-index manifold corollary are then
checked with external geometry and literature results represented as ordinary,
named theorem hypotheses. The Ma--Zheng classification, Vinberg field
identification, Emery--Mila total-reality theorem, Selberg's lemma, and
finite-index trace-field invariance are not claimed as newly verified.

The project contains no project-defined `axiom` or `opaque` declaration and
no `sorry`, `admit`, or `native_decide`.

## Exact certificates

Headline checks and their durable outputs include:

- `scripts/p11_6_7_non_total_trace.py` and
  `results/p11_6_7_non_total_trace_certificate.json`;
- `scripts/p11_8_non_total_trace.py` and
  `results/p11_8_non_total_trace_certificate.json`;
- `scripts/p118_trace_field.py` and
  `results/p118_trace_field_certificate.json`;
- `scripts/p118_field_independent.gp`, an independent full-field PARI/GP
  check; and
- `scripts/p11_8_finite_volume.py` with the exact geometry certificate and
  pinned CoxIter record under `results/`.

The full $P_{11,8}$ field check finds degree $8$, signature $(4,2)$, and
discriminant $40960000=2^{16}5^4$. The direct geometry check finds ten
vertices, two cusps, and finite volume.

The repository also retains supplementary normalized-Clifford and
split-support experiments, census logs, and the conditional Lean proof graph
for that separate criterion. They are not needed for the main non-total-
reality theorem.

## Verify the release candidate

Run the complete local audit:

```sh
python3 scripts/release_check.py
```

Build the manuscript directly:

```sh
cd manuscript
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error \
  non_pseudo_arithmetic_4_manifolds.tex
```

Build Lean after obtaining mathlib's binary cache on a first networked run:

```sh
cd formal
lake exe cache get
lake build
```

Build the deterministic release archive:

```sh
scripts/build_release.sh
```

The source-dependent headline certifiers require pinned copies of the
Ma--Zheng TeX source and the HCPdm incidence data. See
[REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) for URLs, SHA-256 values, serial
commands, and expected conclusions.

non-pseudo-arithmetic lattice in these dimensions.** This reports a targeted
search; it is not an assertion that a finite search proves absolute priority.
