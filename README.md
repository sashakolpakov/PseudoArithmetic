# Non-pseudo-arithmetic hyperbolic 4-lattices

This repository accompanies the manuscript **“Non-Pseudo-Arithmetic
Hyperbolic \(4\)-Manifolds”** by Alexander Kolpakov.  The manuscript is the
source of truth:

- [TeX source](manuscript/non_pseudo_arithmetic_4_manifolds.tex)
- [compiled PDF](manuscript/non_pseudo_arithmetic_4_manifolds.pdf)

We give exact certificates for three finite-volume Coxeter
reflection lattices in $\mathrm{PO}(4,1)$ that are not
pseudo-arithmetic:

$$
P_{11,6},\qquad P_{11,7},\qquad P_{11,8}
$$

from the Ma--Zheng seven-facet census. Their adjoint trace fields contain
$\mathbf Q(\sqrt5,u)$, where

$$
(\sqrt5-1)u^2+2(\sqrt5-3)u+\sqrt5-7=0.
$$

At the conjugate place $\sqrt5\mapsto-\sqrt5$, this polynomial has negative
discriminant $8(1-\sqrt5)$. Thus each adjoint trace field has a complex
place. A pseudo-arithmetic lattice in dimension $>3$ necessarily has a
totally real adjoint trace field, so all three are non-pseudo-arithmetic.
Finite-index torsion-free orientation-preserving subgroups give actual
finite-volume hyperbolic $4$-manifolds with the same obstruction.

The short proof, exact geometric checks, source pins, and novelty audit are
in [the theorem note](notes/p11_6_8_non_total_family.md). This negatively
answers Questions 1.8 and 1.10 of Emery--Mila. A targeted literature search through
4 August 2026 found no previous example; the defensible priority wording is
"we know of no previous non-pseudo-arithmetic lattice in dimension $>3$."

The decisive hand calculation uses only two cyclic products of $A=2G$:

$$
c_{12}=\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2},\qquad
c_{035}=-2\sqrt2\,a.
$$

They put $\sqrt5$ and $u=\sqrt2a$ in the Vinberg cyclic-product field,
which is the adjoint trace field—not merely the larger field generated by
individual Gram entries.

## Formal verification

The pinned Lean 4/mathlib project in [`formal/`](formal/README.md)
kernel-checks the new field-theoretic deduction.  For a number field \(K\)
and \(s,u\in K\), it proves

$$
\mathrm{minpoly}_{\mathbf Q}(s)=X^2-5,\qquad
(s-1)u^2+2(s-3)u+s-7=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
K\text{ is not totally real}.
$$

Lean derives the embedding \(s\mapsto-\sqrt5\) from the minimal polynomial.
The project also checks the normalized-Clifford, ABHN, Hasse–Minkowski, and
pseudo-arithmetic application chains conditionally: the classical inputs
missing from mathlib are explicit theorem hypotheses, not Lean axioms.  The
formalization contains no project-defined `axiom` or `opaque` declaration and
no `sorry`, `admit`, or `native_decide`.  The exact trust boundary is recorded
in [FORMAL_VERIFICATION.md](FORMAL_VERIFICATION.md).

## Lightweight and complete Clifford tests

For rejection alone, determinant normalization and the converse
Hasse--Minkowski argument are unnecessary.  In odd rank the type-`B` Tits
class

$$
t(q)=[C^0(q)]\in\mathrm{Br}(K)[2]
$$

depends only on the similarity class.  If that class descends through a
multiquadratic extension $K/k$, every finite ramified prime of $t(q)$ must
split completely and its entire fibre must ramify.  Thus one nonsplit
ramified prime or incomplete split fibre is an explicit negative witness.  A
pass means only that this finite obstruction vanishes.

`scripts/lightweight_clifford_support.gp` applies this one-way test directly
to an unnormalized Gram representative; it does not load the full descent
implementation.  Its controls cover both failure modes, a nonempty passing
support, similarity invariance, and stabilized ranks $5,7,9,11$.  The
fresh-worker harness `scripts/lightweight_clifford_crosscheck.py` agrees with
all 37 earlier finite-criterion computations; see
`results/lightweight_clifford_crosscheck.jsonl`.  The present executable
witness wrapper is specialized to `K/Q`, exactly matching that census
regression.  A general pseudo-arithmeticity search must apply the same local
degree and orbit test separately to every eligible `K/K^H`; testing only
`K/Q` is not an all-candidate decision procedure.

Independently of the Coxeter counterexamples, the project proves a finite
similarity-descent criterion for odd-rank quadratic forms. For a
multiquadratic extension $K/k$ and odd-rank $q/K$, normalize

$$
q^\#=\langle\det(q)^{-1}\rangle q.
$$

Then $q$ similarity-descends to $k$ exactly when its normalized real
signatures descend and $[C^0(q^\#)]$ is restricted from
$\mathrm{Br}(k)[2]$. At finite places, ramification must be a union of
complete fibers over primes splitting completely in $K/k$. Ramification at
any nonsplit prime is an immediate obstruction.

The statement and proof are in
`notes/split_support_criterion.md`. The rank-five countermodel over
$\mathbf Q(\sqrt5)$ stabilizes in every odd rank at least five, so adding
variables does not force the split condition. The four rank-mod-eight cases
are checked by `scripts/stable_obstruction.gp`.

Before the two-distance breakthrough, all 19 zero-distance and 76
one-distance Ma--Zheng cases received exact positive pseudo-arithmeticity
certificates. The three counterexamples are the first three exactly resolved
two-distance cases in the obstruction search. Every expensive computation is
run in a fresh process; observed case-worker peaks are below $100$ MB.

The 2026 novelty baseline is M. Belolipetsky, N. Bogachev, A. Kolpakov, and
L. Slavich, *Subspace stabilisers in hyperbolic lattices*, J. Assoc. Math.
Res. 4 (2026), 111--182, [arXiv:2105.06897](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06897).

## Quick verification

Build the paper:

```sh
cd manuscript
latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error \
  non_pseudo_arithmetic_4_manifolds.tex
```

Build the Lean development after obtaining mathlib's binary cache:

```sh
cd formal
lake exe cache get
lake build
```

The exact arithmetic checks are deliberately run one at a time.  See
[REPRODUCIBILITY.md](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) for source pins, commands, expected
outputs, and the bounded-memory order of execution.
