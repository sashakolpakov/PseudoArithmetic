# Release notes: 1.0.0-rc1

Date: 24 September 2026

This is the first publication-oriented release candidate for
*Non-Pseudo-Arithmetic Hyperbolic 4-Manifolds*.

## Headline result

The release gives exact certificates that the reflection lattices of the
Ma--Zheng polytopes $P_{11,6}$, $P_{11,7}$, and $P_{11,8}$ have non-totally-
real adjoint trace fields and hence are not pseudo-arithmetic. Finite-index
subgroups give orientable, noncompact, finite-volume hyperbolic
$4$-manifolds with the same obstruction.

## What changed from 0.1.0

- The manuscript grew from a short seven-page announcement to a 19-page,
  self-contained treatment.
- The introduction now defines every specialized concept used later:
  hyperbolic lattices, Coxeter polytopes, Gram matrices, number-field
  embeddings, total reality, adjoint trace fields, ambient groups,
  pseudo-arithmeticity, and cyclic products.
- The paper includes the complete three Gram matrices, a line-by-line radical
  elimination, the embedding-extension argument, detailed theorem and
  corollary proofs, exact-computation notes, and a normative external-input
  register.
- `formal/PseudoArithmetic/Manuscript.lean` now mirrors the paper. It proves
  the concrete nested-radical identity and the internal main/corollary logic,
  while literature results remain typed local hypotheses.
- Repository documentation now uses one title and one explicit trust
  boundary.
- A release validator, deterministic packaging script, version file,
  machine-readable manifest, and executed pre-release checklist were added.

## Verification boundary

Lean unconditionally checks the new algebraic content and uses mathlib's
existing number-field embedding theorem. It does not newly verify the
Ma--Zheng classification, Vinberg's field theorem, Emery--Mila's
total-reality consequence, Selberg's lemma, or hyperbolic quotient geometry.
Each of these appears as a named local hypothesis matching the manuscript's
external-input register.

## Reproduce

From the repository root:

```sh
python3 scripts/release_check.py
scripts/build_release.sh
```

The first command performs the complete offline local audit. The second
creates `dist/PseudoArithmetic-1.0.0-rc1.tar.gz` and its SHA-256 sidecar. The
source-dependent headline calculations can additionally be regenerated by
following `REPRODUCIBILITY.md` after downloading the pinned source inputs.

## Known scope limits

- The release formalizes the manuscript's internal logic, not the external
  classification and field-of-definition literature.
- The stored certificates make the source-derived outputs inspectable
  offline; a fresh end-to-end source parse requires the separately downloaded
  pinned Ma--Zheng and HCPdm files.
- The normalized-Clifford criterion remains a supplementary conditional
  development and is not used by the headline theorem.
